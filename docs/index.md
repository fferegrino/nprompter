# Welcome to Nprompter

> **nprompter** is a browser-based teleprompter powered by Notion as a backend to edit and store your scripts.

<iframe width="560" height="315" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/mEcgO0_kJzk" title="YouTube video player" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen></iframe>

_nprompter_ is ideal for content creators, speakers, educators, and video producers who want a distraction-free, highly readable teleprompter while writing and organizing scripts within their existing Notion workspaces.
